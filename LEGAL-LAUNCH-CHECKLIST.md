# Domenica Yoga — legal launch checklist

Before accepting payments for yoga / movement services through the Ukrainian sole proprietor:

1. Add KVED 85.51 — "Освіта у сфері спорту та відпочинку". The official KVED description explicitly includes yoga instruction and professional sports instructors/trainers.
2. Confirm that the public legal information matches the current ЄДР record after the KVED update.
3. The public legal page currently uses the registered address and taxpayer number from the uploaded ЄДР extract because Article 7 of the Law of Ukraine "On Electronic Commerce" requires direct, simple and stable access to this information for electronic commerce.
4. The uploaded extract gives the registered address but does not establish a separate "actual residence" address. Ukrainian law uses wording that refers to place of registration and actual residence for an individual entrepreneur. If the actual residence differs and you want to minimise disclosure, obtain Ukrainian legal advice on the exact disclosure requirement before taking online payments.
5. The site intentionally uses noindex,follow on Legal / Privacy / Terms pages. This reduces search-engine indexing but does not make the information private; the pages remain publicly accessible from the footer.
6. No advertising or behavioural analytics cookies are currently described. If Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or another non-essential tracker is added later, review cookie/consent requirements and update the privacy policy before enabling it.
